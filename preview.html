Business Explain

eCRM is a centralized platform designed to manage consumer data, marketing activities, survey systems, product insights, and campaign execution using a structured data-driven workflow. Smoking advertisements are prohibited in public media, therefore the organization collects consumer insights through direct outlet interactions using RAs (Retail Agents). All collected data is stored centrally for: consumer analytics, product analysis, competitor analysis, campaign planning, report generation, and business intelligence.

Why This Business Exists

Smoking advertisements are restricted in public media. Therefore, BATB reaches consumers through direct field engagement. Retail Agents visit outlets, interact with consumers, conduct surveys, collect insights, and upload campaign data to the centralized eCRM system.

Campaign Workflow

Campaign → RA Assignment → Outlet Visit → Consumer Interaction → Survey Collection → Contact Submission → Supervisor Validation → Reporting → Analytics → BigQuery Dashboard.

Location Hierarchy

Region → Area → Territory → House → Point → Route → Cluster → Outlet. All consumer interactions occur at the outlet level.

JML Process

PTR System

PTR means Purchase Through Retail. PTR represents rewards, gifts, or promotional items provided during campaigns. PTR data is stored inside contacts table.

Analytics Architecture

SQL Reports

Production-grade reporting system using PostgreSQL queries, pivot logic, survey mapping, cross-schema joins, campaign analytics, consumer verification, and reporting pipelines.

Daily Contact Survey Report

Core reporting query based on ecrm.contacts. Includes consumer mapping, brand mapping, location hierarchy, campaign details, PTR calculation, and survey pivot transformation.

Production SQL 
SELECT
    c.id AS "Contact ID",
    c.contact_date,
    con.name AS "Consumer Name",
    con.contact_no,
    loc.region,
    loc.area,
    loc.territory,
    loc.cluster,
    loc.outlet
FROM ecrm.contacts c

LEFT JOIN ecrm.consumers con
ON c.consumer_id = con.id

LEFT JOIN ecrm.all_locations_region_to_outlet loc
ON c.location_id = loc.outlet_id;
